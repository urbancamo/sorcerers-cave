\[3\] EACH ROUND IS FOUGHT:

**\[Step a\] The Scroll Option**

*{Note: “Only when your party is attacking” - Question: Only before the first round if you choose the surprise bonus option, or before any round if you choose the “grenade” ??”*

1. IF (An attacking player party includes a creature able to use Scroll) AND (Eye-of-God is not present) THEN (The player decides whether or not to use Scroll).
2. IF (Scroll is used) THEN (The player decides which variant of Scroll to invoke.):

    1. Destroy all enemies in this area that do not have innate magical power (i.e. fighters).
    2. Double the party’s first round surprise-attack bonus.
    3. Add a party first round surprise-attack bonus in a scenario where it would otherwise be absent.

3. The attacking party is then cursed.

*{Note: Enemies destroyed by Scroll drop anything they were carrying. Sybil is not destroyed. An invulnerable stranger bearing the Ring on level 4 disappears along with the Ring. Magic Shield does not protect from Scroll.}*

1.  **\[Step b\] Defender Prepares Their Defense**

*{Note: If this is not the first round, there may be one or more unresolved matches still in play, with creatures still* ***engaged****.}*

-   IF  (The attacker and defender have **unengaged** creatures) THEN (The defender deploys unengaged creatures into the front line. Any creatures that are deployed in this way are in the front line, but not yet engaged in a match. The defender must attempt to deploy at least as many creatures as the attacker has unengaged creatures.)
-   IF (The defender has **more** unengaged creatures than the attacker) THEN (The defender may keep back surplus creatures for later deployment.)
-   IF (The defender has **fewer** unengaged creatures than the attacker) THEN (All of the defender's unengaged creatures must deploy to the front line to meet the attack).

-   **\[Step c\] Attacker Creates Engagement (Matches)**

-   IF (The defender has **unengaged** creatures in the front line) THEN (The attacker pairs up their unengaged creatures against the defender front line unengaged creatures one-to-one, thereby creating a one or more new **matches.** This proceeds until all of the defender's front line are **engaged** in a match, or until no more new **matches** are possible.)

-   **\[Step d\] Deployment is Finalised**

*{Note: This step implements rigorously the somewhat vague as-written rule “.. he must fight the strongest combination.}*

IF (One party still has **unengaged** creatures) THEN:

-   The larger party’s creatures that were **already engaged** in the front line of a match at the start of this round must remain where they are, and may not be redeployed.

-   The larger party’s **remaining unengaged** creatures, and all creatures fighting in the **background,** may now be redeployed into any position that they are allowed to adopt - provided that the number of matches is not changed, and the number of creatures in the front line of any match does not exceed two.

-   IF (all matches now involve two creatures fighting in the front line) AND (The larger party still has **fighter** creatures unengaged) THEN (Those fighters must stay out of the fight).

-   Any creature in the larger party that the player does not wish to be engaged stays out of the fight.

-   **\[Step e\] Each Match in the Round is Fought**

-   The round is fought, match by match, until all matches have been fought. *{See Fighting A Match*}.

-   **\[Step f\] Wrap up the Round**

IF (One side has no creatures able to fight) THEN (Then the fight is over.) *{See: Ending a Fight}*

ELSE:

-   Any match in which one side has no creatures in the front line is dissolved and all creatures that were engaged in it become **unengaged**.
-   All creatures in the background become **unengaged**.
-   Any creatures still engaged with the enemy **in the front line** of a match remain where they are.

IF (One party wishes to retreat) AND (They are not a stranger party) THEN (they may now attempt to retreat. *{See: Ending a Fight}*

IF (A party does not successfully retreat, or cannot retreat) THEN (It is now the alternate party’s turn, and another another round is fought.)
