FIGHTING A MATCH:

**\[Step i\] Calculate Effective Strength per Creature.**

**Effective strength** is the strength that the creature is able to contribute to their side of the match, after considering any applicable effects as follows:

1.  1. Positional Effects:

-   Magic users in the background

2.  2. Artefact Effects:

-   Eye-of-God present
-   Magic Axe borne by a creature able to bear it.
-   Magic Shield borne by a creature able to bear it.
-   Magic Staff borne by a creature able to bear it.
-   Magic Sword borne by a creature able to bear it.
-   Talisman on display
-   The Ring borne by a creature able to bear it.

3.  3. Immaterial Creature Effects:

-   Spectre
-   Demon

-   **\[Step ii\] Calculate Total Effective Strength Per Side**

Calculate each side’s total effective strength (TES) by adding the effective strength of each creature engaged in the match.

-   **\[Step iii\] Roll Dice & Calculate Result Per Side**

Roll a single d6 for each side.

**Result** \= ( TES) + ( Die Roll ) + ( First Round Surprise Attack Bonus {0, 1, 2} ) + ( Ring Bonus {0, 1} )

-   **\[Step iiii\] Determine Match Outcome**

The possible outcomes of a match are:

-   **Stalemate**: Both side’s result is zero.
-   **Tie**: Both side’s results are numerically equal but non-zero.
-   **Win**: One side’s result is higher than the other.

IF (The match outcome is a **tie**, or a **stalemate**) THEN (The match is **unresolved.)**

ELSE (The match result  is a **win.** There is a winning side and a losing side)

(IF (The losing side in this round had 2 creatures in the front line THEN:

-   The losing party nominates one front-line creature to become a casualty.
-   A die is rolled (+1 if the losing party has the Ring, 7 counts as 6). On 4, 5, 6, the loser gets their choice and that creature dies.
-   The dead creature is removed from play, but the small card remains in the area for now (in case healing balm can be applied after the fighting is over).
-   The match is **unresolved.**

When a stranger party has to nominate one out two of its front line creatures as a casualty, the game passes that choice to the winning player. A die is rolled (+1 if the losing party has the Ring, 7 counts as 6). On 4, 5, 6, the **other** (not nominated) creature dies. *{Note: This is a work-around, to avoid the game having to make a choice that is not easily computable, and involves subjective assessment of future benefits.}*

ELSE IF (The losing side in this round had 1 creature in the front line) THEN (The match is **resolved**.)

-   That creature dies and is removed from play, but the small card remains in the area for now (in case healing balm can be applied after the fighting is over).
