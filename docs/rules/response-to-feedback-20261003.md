  - ***Re. Eye of God***

Eye of god.

"Artefact. A gem which renders powerless all magic in the area, and
annihilates SPECTRES and

ZOMBIES. Having taken it up, you must keep it, otherwise you will be
CURSED."

"Priests, witches, scholar, apprentice and wizards, and all other
artefacts are powerless **when this**

**gem is in the same area** (priests, witches, scholar, apprentice and
wizards retain fighting strength);

spectres and zombies are permanently destroyed. The statue bearing the
lost ruby is powerless to

attack. Once a party has taken up the Eye, the gem must be kept with the
main body of the party;

otherwise there is a curse on that party until the gem is again taken up
by the same or another

party. This applies even if the gem is left behind involuntarily, e.g.
on the body of a slain creature.

Note that on encountering the Eye your party is under no obligation to
pick it up."

I think the interpretation that it *does not need to be borne* is
reinforced by contrasting this wording with the wording used in relation
to Talisman:

Talisman. 10 vp.

"Artefact. Wards of ZOMBIES and GHOULS from the party displaying it. On
the fourth level or deeper, also wards off SPECTRES."

"**It has no warding-off power until actually taken up** by an exploring
party; i.e. it may be guarded by ghouls or spectres. When warded off,
ghouls and spectres cease to have any effect as long as the party with
the talisman remains in the area. The party possessing it may use its
power to protect other parties in the same area."

- **(Also in Options and Variations -- Hidden Cards):**

"Players need keep on display in their parties only their creature cards
and any artefact which is being used. (It is wise to **keep the talisman
on display** so that ghouls and spectres can be passed by without
comment.)"

Talisman explicitly does not work unless being borne by a creature. So
Mr. Donnelly could easily have included that phrasing for Eye of God if
it was appropriate. I conclude that the Eye of God is "always on"
affecting the area it is currently in.

- **Counter Argument:**

Interestingly however, if you were to apply D&D lore in this situation
the answer would be the exact opposite in both cases!

A talisman does not need to be displayed for its deterrent to have
effect, which is radiated effect. It can be safely tucked into a shirt.
The Eye is a magical gem. In D&D, such gems almost always need to be
displayed in some way, or some action taken (such as shattering it to
release the entity trapped inside\...) in order to activate the magic.

But that\'s a different rule system\...

- ***Re: Thief***

Ah very interesting\...

I made an error in the Default Loadout -- but not for the reason Claude
thinks\.....!

This highlights a more general issue with the rules as written in the
booklets and on the cards.

Mr. Donnelly had to deal with the fact that the base game was already in
existence when the extension came out. So, Magic Sword and Magic Axe
have text on them from the base set -- and that was decided before there
was such a thing as a Thief.

So the game cannot make the decision as to who can use which artefact
based only on the artefact card text. It must also consider the creature
card text. (In this case Thief: "Uses artefacts as a Man")

i.e.: If a Man can use the Sword, Axe, or Shield, then a Thief can too.

Same goes for (Witch: "Uses artefacts as a Priest").

and (Scholar: "Uses artefacts as a Priest").

and (Apprentice: "Uses artefacts as a Wizard").

We may (?) also have a related problem with Woman Hero, because her card
text does not read *"uses artefacts as a Hero"*. Instead the booklet
rules text reads *"The woman-hero has all the capabilities of a woman
and a hero."*

So I suspect there may be a systemic bug due not implementing "*uses
artefacts as*" and "*has all the capabilities of*" correctly.

Now to my error -- given that Thief and Woman have identical fighting
strength and can use the same artefacts, the only reason to rank one
higher in preference for artefact loadout is other abilities. The
Thief\'s special ability is of no use to strangers. The Woman\'s special
ability is:

**Unicorn**. STR 4. CAP 0 kg. 4 vp.

*"Friendly to WOMEN, otherwise indifferent."*

If found with a woman, it will remain loyal to her.

I interpret the Unicorn rules as meaning:

IF (a stranger party is approached and the outcome is "hostile") AND
(The stranger party includes a Woman) AND (The stranger party includes a
Unicorn) THEN ( The Unicorn will fight for the stranger party.)

So the Woman\'s special ability is useful.

I have changed the preference lists to place Woman higher than Thief.
