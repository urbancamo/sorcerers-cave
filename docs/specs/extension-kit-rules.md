# Extension Kit Rules

> OCR transcription of `extension-kit-rules.png` (2026-07-26).

THIS EXTENSION KIT is made for use with THE SORCERER'S CAVE (A Game of Exploration, Magic, and
Adventure), copyright 1978 by Terence Donnelly and published by Philmar Limited.

Many new cards are added to both packs, and the size of the Cave is increased by half. But the
rules of play remain as in the original game.

Most of the cards are self-explanatory, but some require further explanation as given under
NOTES ON THE CARDS, below.

We suggest that where time or space is limited, or new players are being introduced to the game,
you remove a third to a half of the large pack before beginning play. In this way the game will
remain small but new things from the extension kit will appear.

## INVENTORY

30 area cards, including chasm, whirlpool, bell rope, well, lair, and gallery.

30 small cards, comprising the following:

| Creatures | Heavy treasure | Artefacts | Hazards |

Finally, a number of cardboard counters are included to serve the same purpose as the small
plastic markers in the basic game. These are numbered to make plainer the connections between
levels of the Cave.
