# 20261030 Response to UI Requirements Specification

## Card View.

"Any card" - I didn't cover this in the presentation, but I had intended this to mean literally every
card that you can click on – including large cards.

When you stand in the doorway of a newly revealed card, the "Encounter" version of the Card View
automatically displays – but also:

When you click on a revealed area card that you are not currently scouting, the RHS shows a
landscape "mini" representation of the area card, with a suitable textual description.

Initially this could be terse except for special chambers – but if we choose to go for the "full on"
story narrative approach, then below are a few examples of the style in which an area card might be
annotated in the Card View:
Fogged
"You have not yet explored that area."
Revealed Tunnel
"A tunnel, with exits North, West and East."
Revealed Tunnel With Demon
"A tunnel, with exits North and South. Something evil lurks in the darkness..."
Previously Revealed Empty Chamber
"A chamber with exits West, East and South, and a secret stairway leading upwards."
Previously Revealed Non-Empty Chamber
"A chamber with exits North, West, East. There are strangers here – a Wizard and a Woman. They
are guarding some silver. You recall withdrawing from the entrance - they did not see you."
Previously Visited Non-Empty Chamber
"A chamber with exits North, West, East. You recall approaching the strangers here – a Wizard and
a Woman, guarding some silver. They were indifferent to you."
Revealed Special Chamber
"Dark water churns here – there are reachable exits to the West and East – but crossing the shallows
risks the pull of the whirlpool."
...and so on – but (as a suggestion) - only if the player chooses the "verbose descriptions" option?
