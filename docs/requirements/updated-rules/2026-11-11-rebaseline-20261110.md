## What changed in the rules text

Only two lines of the rules PDF: the party-selection example ("a troll and a man, or a priest and two dwarves") and the Magic Staff card ("a PRIEST by 1, or WIZARD by 2"). The rest is word for word as 20261005.

## The tables: U / C / T instead of ticks

The old "y" tables of 20261006 are replaced by a three-letter key. **U** = can carry and use (bear, wield, wear, display, administer); **C** = can carry but not use; **T** = can be the target of the effect. A blank means the creature cannot carry the item at all. This corrects my own reading of 20261006: I took a blank for "may not carry", and the four rulings of 2026-11-10 (Eye of God, Scroll, Talisman, Elixir "for humans and magic users only") were built on it. Peter's feedback #6 says exactly that "may use" and "may carry" are different things.

| Item | Before (2026-11-10) | Now (the key) |
|---|---|---|
| Eye of God | humans and magic users only; an inhuman's does nothing | **C for every creature that can carry it; it works by being carried.** An Ogre's, Dwarf's, Giant's or Troll's Eye counts again |
| Talisman | worn by a human or magic user | same: U for humans and magic users; the Dwarf, Giant, Ogre and Troll may carry it (C) but it does nothing there |
| Scroll | read by a human or magic user carrier | same (U); C for the Dwarf, Giant, Ogre and Troll |
| Elixir | drunk only by a human or magic user | **a human or magic user (U) gives it to ANY living member (T)**, an Ogre or a Lion included. Dwarf, Giant, Ogre and Troll may carry it (C) |
| Strength Potion | any holder, drinkers Hero, Woman Hero, Man, Thief, Woman | the holder who gives it must be one of those five (U); the magic users and the four inhumans carry it only (C) |
| Lion, Unicorn, Wolf | could carry artefacts (they weigh nothing) | **carry none** (blank in every row), except as targets of the Elixir and the Healing Balm. The Lion card says the same |

Everything else is as it was: the Sword, Axe, Shield, Staff, Ring, Carpet, Flute, Holy Water, Lotus Dust and the Healing Balm users.

## Engine changes

- `effects.ts`: `canUseArtifact` is the U test for 7, 15, 19 (humans and magic users) and now 8 (`potionEligible`); not for 13. `partyHolds` (the Eye) accepts any living holder again.
- `pickup.ts`: `canCarry` refuses an artefact to the Lion, Unicorn and Wolf.
- `selectors.ts`, `reduce.ts`: the Elixir needs a U holder and is given to any living member.
- `data/creatures.ts`: **Ogre cost 4, Troll cost 3 in the base game** (feedback #4). `KIT_COST_OVERRIDES` removed. 37 legal base drafts (was 31).

Deserters leave with what they carry and mutineers' goods go to the floor of the area (feedback #11): the engine already did both. The Staff already gave the Priest +1.

## Tests

`rulebookArtefacts.test.ts` now parses the U / C / T tables and checks every cell for use, carry and target (and the Eye's carried effect): 682 checks, 3 known differences. The three are the Healing Balm row, where the Hero, Man and Thief are T only with no C; the engine lets them carry it. A likely missing C in Peter's table; to ask him (R-34).

Engine 128 files, 2623 passed, 42 skipped; web 93 files, 1244 passed, 8 skipped.
