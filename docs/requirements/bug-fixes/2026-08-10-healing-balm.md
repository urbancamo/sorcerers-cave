I have played a House Rule for Healing Balm for quite a while, and it seems to work quite well.

I believe Healing Balm is just a simple "health potion". If this were D&D, the equivalent scenario would be a party member is on their final Death Save (hovering on the point of death), and if someone doesn't give them 1 Hit Point immediately, then it's potentially very bad news.

So I would re-write the clarification rule as:

"It may be applied to a fallen party member only at the beginning of a turn when any fighting is over, only in the first such turn subsequent to the death, and only if the party has not moved from the area where the death occurred."

So:
You can't use it in between rounds of fighting.
It does cover those who fell in a fight that you eventually won.
It does not include those who died in a fight from which you retreated.
You have a single turn, very limited window of opportunity to use it.
It does potentially cover a creature who was poisoned by Elixir.

I believe this restricted interpretation is consistent with Healing Balm having no point value. A much more powerful potion would surely have point value (see Lotus Dust) ...?
