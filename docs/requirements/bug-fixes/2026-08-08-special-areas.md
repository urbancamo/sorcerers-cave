# Chasm, Deep Pool, Viper Pit, Whirlpool

Proposals for rules revisions for the app 2026-08-06 PV – consideration...

I have tried to approach the game implementation by asking “What are the minimal changes to the
original rules necessary to explain how the app works ?

## Deep Pool

### ORIGINAL:

On first turning the card over you move your token onto it, but not across the water. On the
following turn you may either move back to the area you were last in or cross the water and proceed
through any doorway. If a giant has to carry across more than one load of heavy treasure there is a
delay of one turn per additional load before the party can proceed.

Heavy treasure that has to be left behind is left in the doorway. Treasure may be deliberately cast
into the pool, and is recoverable only by a giant.

A stairway or trap leading to the deep pool comes out on an island in the middle. Treasure may be
left on this island.

### REVISED:

On first turning the card over you move your token onto it, but not across the water. On the
following turn you may either move back to the area you were last in or cross the water and proceed
through any doorway, including any secret door that you may have discovered on the island. If a
giant has to carry across more than one load of heavy treasure there is a delay of one turn per
additional load before the party can proceed.

If a non-giant crosses carrying heavy treasure, or if you voluntarily drop treasure here, it sinks to
the bottom of the pool, and is recoverable only by a giant.

A stairway or trap leading to the deep pool comes out on the island in the middle.

## Viper Pit

### ORIGINAL:

On first turning the card over you move your tokens onto it, but not onto the narrow ledge. On the
following turn, you may either go back to the area you were last in or cross one segment of the
narrow ledge and, if you wish, try to leave by that doorway. To cross another segment and proceed
through that doorway requires another turn. Any treasure carried by a creature who falls off the
ledge remains in the pit, and is recoverable only by a party with the charmed flute. Treasure may be
deliberately cast into the pit.

A stairway or trap leading to the viper pit comes out on a safe island in the middle.

### REVISED:

On first turning the card over you move your tokens onto it, but not onto the narrow ledge. On the
following turn, you may either go back to the area you were last in or cross one segment of the
narrow ledge and, if you wish, try to leave by that doorway. If you have discovered a secret
doorway on the island, you may jump across to the island and leave by that stair. To cross another
segment of the ledge (or to jump from the island) and proceed through a different doorway requires
another turn. Any treasure carried by a creature who falls off the ledge, or who falls into the pit
while jumping, remains in the pit, and is recoverable only by a party with the charmed flute.
Treasure dropped here voluntarily goes into the pit.

A stairway or trap leading to the viper pit comes out on the island in the middle.

## Chasm

### ORIGINAL:

On the turn following entry you may either go back the way you came in, or descend to the next
level. A stairway up or down to the chasm leads to the island or column in the middle, from where
the party can only descend, except to trace its steps up a stair. A trap to here sends you down a
further level.

### REVISED:

On first turning the card over you move your token onto it, but not across the chasm. On the turn
following entry you may either go back the way you came in, or descend to the next level. A
stairway up or down to the chasm leads to the island or column in the middle, from where the party
can only descend, except to retrace its steps up a stair. A trap to here sends you down a further
level.

You may not drop treasure into the chasm. (see issues later).

## Whirlpool

### ORIGINAL:

Basically like the viper pit, except that you roll only once for the entire party. Any stairway leading
to this area is considered blocked. A trap to this area sends you down another level.

### REVISED:

Basically like the viper pit, except that you roll only once for the entire party.

On first turning the card over you move your token onto it, but not into the Whirlpool. On the
following turn, you may either go back to the area you were last in or cross one segment of the
shallows and, if you wish, try to leave by that doorway. To cross another segment of the shallows,
and proceed through a different doorway requires another turn.

Any stairway leading to this area is considered blocked. A trap to this area sends you down another
level.

You may not drop treasure into the Whirlpool. (see issues later).

## Unresolved Issues:

I'm not at all happy with simply prohibiting the dropping of treasure in Chasm and Whirlpool. It
seems arbitrary and difficult to rationalise in the in-game universe. In multiplayer, you can't fight
(which may imply dropping heavy treasure) in the Chasm or the Deep Pool – but Whirlpool is not
mentioned. On the other hand, in the original you can voluntarily drop treasure at the edge of the
Deep Pool – so the rules are inconsistent.

Since artefacts stolen by Harpies go to a temporary holding area if LAIR is not yet in play, would
it make sense to utilise a similar procedure for Chasm & Whirlpool – i.e. Treasure dropped in the
chasm or the Whirlpool falls to the next level, and goes to a temporary holding area if the area
below is not yet in play...?
