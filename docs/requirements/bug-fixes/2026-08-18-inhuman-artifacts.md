There are some bug fixes required around the carrying and use of artifacts by non-human users.

Human Creature classes (from [Expanded Consolidated Rules PV2026.md](../../other/Expanded%20Consolidated%20Rules%20PV2026.md)): 

 - Man
 - Woman
 - Thief

 - Woman Hero
