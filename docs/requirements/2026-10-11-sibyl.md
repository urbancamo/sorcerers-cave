# Sibyl

The Sibyl is an optional card in the game. These are the details on the card:

## Notes

Remains neutral in fights, invulnerable.

## Stranger Test Roles

1-4 Indifferent
5-6 Friendly

## Additional Notes

Test after other STRANGERS have been dealt with, and only once.

If you have a SIBYL to guide you, you may examine any area card at
the beginning of your turn, including the top card of the pack.

She will not leave the cave.


## Implementation

I'm not sure how the text *you may examine any area card at
the beginning of your turn* should be interpreted.

The first step is to ensure we have a good interpretation of the card in terms
of changes to the rules, and a decision on how this should be implemented.

Sibyl will need to be another variant of the game in terms of keeping high scores,
as when she is in the pack the game result can be very different.
