# Neural Net Based Stranger Pairing

I'd like to investigate the possibility of a side-project to implement a neural network
based stranger pairing system.

Stranger pairing here means choosing the best pairing of strangers to a players party during
combat. The inputs to the neural network would be as follows:

 - the party members of strangers
 - the artifacts available to the strangers
 - the party members of the player
 - the artifacts being wielded by the player's party members

Initially, I'm interested in what software is available in the open source domain to allow
a neural network to be trained, and then used by the game. Ideally, initially, I'd like to
train the network on my MacBook, and then provide it as a service on a linux machine accessible
to convex - unless there is a simpler solution.

Create a feasibility document. Assume that I know nothing about training or using
custom AI models, but do have a general understanding on how they work.
