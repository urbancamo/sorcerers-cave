Ring Rules:

The Ring. 30 vp.
“Artefact. When worn by a MAN, WOMAN, PRIEST, WIZARD, HERO or DWARF, adds 1 to the die rolls of your party. Also makes the bearer invincible on the fourth level or deeper.”
The bonus value of the ring applies to all die rolls in a round of fighting, even if the bearer is slain in that round. On the fourth level or deeper the bearer fights in the normal way, but die rolls which would normally indicate his death are ignored. On being defeated an invulnerable stranger will disappear with the ring, leaving other treasure behind.

This creates 2 puzzles as follows:

(1) Literal Contradiction.
There is no clear definition of the word "invincible", which (etymologically) means "cannot be defeated".
But later, the text reads "On being defeated....".

(2) Omission.
"On the fourth level or deeper the bearer fights in the normal way, but die rolls which would normally indicate his death are ignored."

So a stranger ringbearer on Level 4 follows all of the rules of normal fighting - with one exception: "...die rolls which would normally indicate their death are ignored."

"..fights in the normal way" very definitely includes normal non-magic users being destroyed by Scroll, as per the Fighting Rules, and Scroll use is not a die roll.

Mr, Donnelly was quite capable of writing:
"On the fourth level or deeper the bearer fights in the normal way, but any outcomes which would normally indicate his removal from the game are ignored."
BUT - he did not write that.

Conclusions:

I think "invincible" was simply a loose wording, the literal contradiction was not intended, and only spotted by a pedant like me. Meh...

However, I also think it is possible we may have been fundamentally misunderstanding Scroll. Scroll should destroy a stranger non magic-user ringbearer on Level 4, causing them to drop the Ring.

The in-game universe explanation would be that everyone who can bear the Ring gets the luck and protection it affords, but it does not allow a non-magic user on level 4 to withstand Scroll (i.e. Scroll is even more powerful than the Ring - which is why using it curses you.)
