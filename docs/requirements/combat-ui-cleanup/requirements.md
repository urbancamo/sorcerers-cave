# Requirements

The Combat UI layout and usability needs to be improved.
There are a numbrer of issues:

 - the green 'drop' zone is too wide by default
 - with a wide enough screen, two party members in the green zone that have weapons overflow to the right.
 - the title 'YOUR FRONT LINE' clashes with the surprise/they attack line
 - clicking on a party member resizes the pair-up line with a large blank area on the LHS.
 - text size too small

We'll also need to look at making it acceptably reactive as the window size reduces.
