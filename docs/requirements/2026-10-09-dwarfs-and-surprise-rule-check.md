# Rules Check

We need to do a careful check of the current Sorcerer's Cave rules relating to traps.
There are two questions to make a judgment on:

## Allow Dwarfs to Deliberately Trigger a Trap

Should we provide the option to allow parties with a dwarf to deliberately fall through a 
   trap?

## Advantage of Surprise and Traps

Validate the following rule clarification: the first party that falls through a trap
does not have the element of surprise. Subsequent trap uses - either deliberate or 
accidental carry a surprise bonus. 

The argument is, that a physical trap
when setup would have a warning system. The first time the trap is used, the warning system
fires and informs any strangers in the chamber below of the player parties triggering of the
trap. Subsequently, additional triggers of the trap do not provide that warning as the 
trap has already been triggered. 

So we are arguing an interpretation of the rules, that traps
are 'single shot' to clarify whether a surprise bonus should be awarded. Also note that in
both solitaire or multiplayer games that a party may already have entered the destination
chamber and immediately withdrawn. In that case they know what is in the chamber. 

Subsequently, in the game they might want to re-enter the chamber and attack the strangers
to gain treasure, and this may be via a trap, identified by another player party, 
deliberately triggered, with or without a dwarf. This relies on visibility of the 
trap from a multiplayer perspective - something that online interpretation of the board game
already has opinions about.

## Expanded Rules Wording - Trap

Current wording in [Expanded Consolidated Rules PV2026 V2.md](../rules/Expanded%20Consolidated%20Rules%20PV2026%20V2.md) relating to traps:

*“Your entire party goes to the area one level below. Any creatures or treasure found in this
chamber remain here. Continues in effect.”*

*A party which falls down a trap ignores any treasure or creature cards found with it, 
but proceeds normally with the turn in the area below, drawing more cards if this is a 
newly discovered chamber. A party which includes a dwarf may ignore a trap, but not two
traps drawn in the same chamber. If a party guided by a dwarf becomes involved in a fight in
a chamber where there is a trap, and the dwarf is killed, the party falls down the trap as soon
as it tries to leave the chamber.*

## Expanded Rules Wording - Advantage of Surprise

Current wording in [Expanded Consolidated Rules PV2026 V2.md](../rules/Expanded%20Consolidated%20Rules%20PV2026%20V2.md) relating to surprise:

The party or group of strangers which gains the advantage of surprise in a fight adds 1 to all its die rolls in the
first round of the fight.

A party gains the advantage of surprise over strangers when attacking immediately after coming into the chamber by a
doorway or stairway that has not been used before, or when arriving by magic carpet. There is no advantage when the
party has fallen down a trap, unless it has done so deliberately.

Strangers gain the advantage when they attack on being approached.
