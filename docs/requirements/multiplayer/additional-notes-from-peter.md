OK - so obviously a Wizard bearing (i.e. wielding) the Magic Staff should not be affected by Medusa - but your real question was about the possessions etc. of Medusa's target.

Here I can't apply my usual "look to D&D" approach, because Peter wrote an explicit rule:

"Medusa. She only attacks parties actually entering the chamber, not those delayed by dead ends or by strangers. Her victims cannot be moved, but anything they were carrying can be taken from them at the end of the turn, providing the party is not involved in a fight at the time."

The SC rules do not fit with the D&D "Flesh to Stone" effect, wherein everything non-magical that the target was wearing, bearing, or carrying is also petrified.

As I mentioned before, I have been happy playing the game with (in my mind) a distinction between "borne", "displaying", and "carrying". (I'll try to stick to Peter's archaic usage "borne" rather than "wielded"...) So, I have assumed that the SC version of turn-to-stone affects only the living flesh - but that has implications.

So my house rules would be:

You can take from a Stone creature (at any later time, just like normal loot, as easily as if they had been dropped on the floor of the chamber):
 - Charmed Flute
 - Crypt Gems
 - Elixir
 - Gems
- Gold
- Healing Balm
- Holy Water
- Idol
- Lost Ruby
- Lotus Dust
- Magic Axe (carried)
- Magic Staff (carried)
- Magic Sword (carried)
- Magic Shield (carried)
- Ring (carried)
- Scroll
- Silver
- Strength Potion
- Talisman
- Treasure Chest

You can not take from a Stone creature:
- Eye Of God (not applicable, because nobody would be stone anyway...)
- Magic Sword (borne)
- Magic Staff (not applicable - when borne by Wizard or Priest, because the character would not be stone anyway, and no-one else would be bearing it...)
- Magic Axe (borne)
- Magic Shield (borne - and it doesn't affect Medusa)
- Ring (borne i.e. worn on the hand).

If I have missed anything out, well, you get the gist of it. I think the approach is consistent, and fits with Peter's "anything they were carrying..."
