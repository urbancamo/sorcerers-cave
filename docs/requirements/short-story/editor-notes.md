# Editor's notes

A review of `story-draft.md` (1,970 words) against `plan.md` section 7. The edited story is `story.md`
(1,791 words, about three pages). Everything below was changed in `story.md` unless it says otherwise.

## Verdict

The draft has a strong spine: the opening line promises something (*no one left to send*) and the ending
keeps it (*you could ask*); the Flute and the Staff are planted and paid; the loud fight is short and the
quiet one (the statue) is the climax, which is true to the run. It needed work in four places: **facts that
contradicted the game run**, **a theme stated twice**, **a few jokes and clichés that broke the voice**, and a
**middle that turned into a list** of recruits. It was also about 200 words over the length asked for.

## 2. Structure and theme

- **The theme was said twice.** The draft's last paragraph explained the Sorcerer-takes, Order-is-given idea at
  length, after the count had already made it. A reader who has just watched fourteen walk out does not need
  it explained. Cut to a single plain sentence that echoes the first line: *"It had not occurred to him that
  you could ask."*
- **The Eye of God was motivated vaguely** ("not for the reason he would have admitted to"). Made concrete:
  Aldric wants to be *sure* of people, and that is exactly why he leaves it. This also sets up the Mutiny
  (an Eye would have told him Wynne was about to leave, and he would have lost the moment where asking
  worked), and sharpens the Viper Pit payoff: the Eye *tells*; the flute *does*.
- **The Viper Pit's aside was weak.** "The Eye would not have fitted on that ledge" was a joke with no logic
  behind it. Replaced with the knowing-versus-doing line above.
- **The whisper became a motif** instead of a one-off: planted at the Mutiny, *falters* when Aldric speaks,
  and *stops* when Skeldur falls. It gives the Sorcerer a presence before he appears, and makes his death
  land on something the reader has been hearing.
- **The ending now closes the loop on the opening.** The first paragraph ends with the old words for a *dead*
  lamp; the last says them for a lamp that is not. The Ruby lights the wick. That was in the draft in a muddled
  form (the Ruby was in the Wizard's hands, but Aldric lit a lantern "from the Ruby" himself); it now passes
  through the Wizard.

## 4. Length and pace

- 1,970 to 1,791 words, 9% shorter. Cut: the second Wizard; the second explanation of the theme; most of the
  Dragon fall's repeated clauses; the Ghouls' "like a bad idea" simile; the Medusa gag; the "asked twice" and
  "Hero's daughter" clutter in the montage.
- I did not cut more. Each remaining scene is doing one of three jobs (the want, the cost, a payoff); the
  scenes I considered dropping next, and kept, were the Eye and Flute choice (it plants the Flute and
  defines Aldric), the Unicorn (it is the hush after the loss), and the statue (it is the real climax).
- **What the story deliberately leaves out of the run:** the Troll declined on level 2, the Healing Balm,
  the Earthquake chamber (the party attacked two strangers who had not harmed them, which would have needed
  a justification the length does not allow), the Treasure Chest, the second Priest and the extra Men.

## 5. Open points for the author

1. **Names.** Aldric, Wynne, Skeldur, Harrowmere, Calamir and the Order of the Kindled Lamp are invented; the
   rest are named by kind. If you would rather the party were named, the cost is words.
2. **How much Tolkien.** The story takes the *register* and a few echoes (the Elder Days, the light before
   the Sun, a Sorcerer once numbered among the wise who fell to the love of command) and no names, plot or
   text. It can be pushed further, or stripped back to a plain fantasy voice.
3. **The Earthquake chamber** is the one moral shadow in the run (an attack on two strangers). It could be a
   good short scene if the story wanted its hero to be made uneasy by the party's strength; it is out here
   for length.
4. **Whether to credit the run.** The story does not say which game it follows; `plan.md` does (B002, seed
   827430).
