# The Sorcerer's Cave: points in the rulebook that need your attention

> **Answered** by Peter on 2026-11-09 (`docs/rules/20261109 Book Errors Feedback.pdf`); the outcomes, and the engine changes that followed, are in `rulings-register.md` (R-34 to R-40).

Peter,

While checking the game engine line by line against *Expanded Consolidated Rules PV2026 V2*, we found a few places where the book disagrees with itself or with the cards, and a few where it says nothing. Mark has ruled on each for the game as it stands. We would like you to confirm them or correct the book.

## 1. Places where the book contradicts itself

| # | Where | What the book says | The conflict | What the game does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Creature Cards, **Woman Hero** | "Reacts: 1-3 Hostile. 3-6 Friendly." | The roll of 3 appears in both bands. | Treats it as 1-3 Hostile, 4-6 Friendly. Please confirm. |
| 2 | Creature distribution table, **Demon** | One Demon in the base game and one in the extension. | The Demon is an Extension Kit creature. | The Demon exists only with the Extension Kit. |
| 3 | Creature distribution table, **Woman Hero** | Listed only in the "No in Ext" column. | The selection table at "Making up the exploring parties" offers her in the base game. | The Woman Hero is available in the base game. |
| 4 | Selection table (party selection) | Ogre 4, Troll 3, and four Women and four Dwarfs in the pack. | The same page gives "a troll and two dwarves" as an example of a party worth 6. That only works if a Troll costs 4. The distribution table elsewhere gives Women and Dwarfs as 3 in the base game plus 1 in the extension. | The base game uses Ogre 5, Troll 4, three Women and three Dwarfs. The Extension Kit uses Ogre 4, Troll 3, four Women and four Dwarfs. It looks as if the table shows the extension figures and the example shows the base ones. Please say which is intended and, if you like, label both. |
| 5 | Artefact-use table, **The Ring** | The Dwarf column is blank for an allied Dwarf but YES for a stranger Dwarf. | The Ring card says a Dwarf may wear it. | A Dwarf may wear it in either role. We took the blank to be a typo. |
| 6 | Artefact-use table, **Magic Sword and Magic Axe** | YES for Priest, Witch, Scholar, Wizard and Apprentice. | The cards give a fighting bonus only to Man, Woman and Hero, and the Axe a +3 to the Dwarf. | These creatures may carry them but get no bonus. Please confirm "may use" means "may carry". |
| 7 | Artefact-use table, **Strength Potion** | YES for the Thief and the priest class. | The Strength Potion card names only Man, Woman or Hero. | Man, Woman, Hero and Thief may drink it. The priest class may not. Please confirm. |
| 8 | **Magic Staff** | "Increases the magical power of a PRIEST or WIZARD by 2." | The physical card, as Mark reads it, gives the Priest +1 and the Wizard +2. | The game follows the card: Priest +1, Wizard +2. Please say which is right. |
| 9 | **Scroll** | The Scroll "cannot be used twice in one area" and works only when your party is attacking. | Elsewhere the book says items such as the Carpet and Lotus Dust are discarded once used. It does not say whether the Scroll is, and "twice in one area" suggests it is kept. | The revised combat keeps it and allows one use per area. The classic combat uses it up and allows it to either side. Mark has chosen to leave the classic combat unchanged. Please say which you intended. |

## 2. Places where the book says nothing, and what we decided

Please confirm these, or tell us what you intended so the book can say it.

| # | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | When Desertion or Mutiny leaves a party with no original members, or an ally must stay loyal, who chooses? | Whenever the party has no original member left, for any reason, the player picks a new leader from those remaining. That creature then counts as an original member. |
| 11 | Where does a mutineer's or deserter's treasure go? | It is left in the chamber. |
| 12 | Is the Talisman "had" if carried, or must it be worn? (Ghouls and Harpies) | It must be worn. |
| 13 | A Unicorn whose party has no Woman. | It stays loyal only while the party has a Woman. Otherwise it turns indifferent and guards the treasure. If it is the last member left, the party does not survive. |
| 14 | Quarrel: who is chosen when strengths are tied? | The player chooses between equals, counting swords, staffs and axes carried. |
| 15 | Two Earthquakes at once. | In the same chamber they destroy one area. In different chambers they destroy two. |
| 16 | Does the Eye of God protect against Medusa? | Yes. Medusa has no effect while the Eye is present. |
| 17 | **Deep Pool:** what is "one load" for a Giant? | We took it as the Giant's carrying capacity, 150 kg (six 25 kg sacks). Each further load costs the party one turn, and several Giants carry in parallel. |
| 18 | **Dead end:** your (*) marks the end of the turn. | A dead end now uses up the turn. |
| 19 | **Surprise:** "a route you cannot walk back". | Magic Carpet, the Sorcerer's teleport, a Whirlpool drag, the Chasm and a deliberate trap fall always give surprise. So does a doorway not used before, even into a chamber already visited. |
| 20 | **Leader of mixed strangers.** | Your list is followed exactly, so no two creatures tie except Hero with Woman Hero, and Demon with Spectre. |

## 3. A note on how these were found

Every creature card, reaction table, selection table and artefact-use table in the book was read by program and compared with the game's data. Those tables now agree with the game apart from the points above. Each item has a test in the project that will fail if the game and your ruling ever drift apart.
